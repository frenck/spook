Pairs of English and the Spanish it should become. They are here for the tone: short sentences, "tú", and the words Home Assistant itself uses.

| English                                                                                           | Spanish                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spook reported a problem, and you want it gone.                                                   | Spook ha avisado de un problema y quieres quitártelo de encima.                                    |
| You don't have to mark anything as done.                                                          | No tienes que marcar nada como hecho.                                                              |
| Nothing there? Then your setup is clean, or Spook is still looking.                               | ¿No hay nada? Entonces tu instalación está limpia, o Spook sigue buscando.                         |
| An automation that stopped working usually does so without a word. Spook tells you.               | Una automatización que deja de funcionar no suele avisar. Spook sí.                                |
| That is a bit late.                                                                               | Eso es un poco tarde.                                                                              |
| It happens, and it is a bug.                                                                      | Pasa, y es un fallo.                                                                               |
| A bug report without logs is a guessing game.                                                     | Un informe de fallo sin registros es jugar a las adivinanzas.                                      |
| A repair that cries wolf is a bug.                                                                | Una reparación que da falsas alarmas es un fallo.                                                  |
| The repair disappears by itself once it is fixed.                                                 | La reparación desaparece sola en cuanto está resuelto.                                             |
| When that area does not exist, it raises a repair that names the automation and the missing area. | Si esa área no existe, Spook crea una reparación que nombra la automatización y el área que falta. |
| Select **Save**.                                                                                  | Selecciona **Guardar**.                                                                            |
| Go to **Settings** > **Devices & services**.                                                      | Ve a **Configuración** > **Dispositivos y servicios**.                                             |
| No files to copy, no YAML.                                                                        | Sin archivos que copiar, sin YAML.                                                                 |
| Like the guest mode you switch on in December and find again in March.                            | Como el modo invitados que activas en diciembre y vuelves a encontrar en marzo.                    |
| An alarm nobody hears is just furniture.                                                          | Una alarma que nadie oye es solo un mueble.                                                        |
| Spook doesn't mind.                                                                               | A Spook no le importa.                                                                             |

What these show:

- The subject pronoun goes: "quieres", "tienes", not "tú quieres".
- Spook takes no article. "Spook sí." is the whole answer to "Spook tells you.": short beats complete.
- "Raises a repair" is "crea una reparación". A trigger "se activa". An automation is "activada" or "desactivada".
- "Setup" is "instalación", "issue" is "problema", "the logs" are "los registros", because that is what Home Assistant calls them in Spanish.
- A question opens with "¿", every time.
