Italian, as written in Italy, the way one Home Assistant user explains something to another.

## Address

- Informal, always: "tu", "tuo", "tua", and the verb in the second person singular. Never "Lei" or "voi". Home Assistant's Italian interface says "tu" too ("Segui il collegamento", "puoi"), and so do Spook's own Italian strings.
- Instructions in the imperative singular: "Vai in **Impostazioni**", "Seleziona **Salva**".
- "We" stays "noi", mostly in the verb only. Frenck speaks as "io" where the English has "I" or "my", again mostly in the verb.
- Leave out the subject pronoun where Italian leaves it out: "Puoi", not "Tu puoi", unless it is emphasis.

## Tone

- Short sentences. Italian invites long periods with "il quale" and three subordinate clauses: do not accept the invitation.
- No bureaucratic Italian: "procedere a", "effettuare", "al fine di", "in merito a", "si prega di", "qualora". Write "fare", "per", "su", "se", the plain verb.
- No noun chains where a verb works: "per riparare l'automazione", not "per l'effettuazione della riparazione dell'automazione".
- Dry humor stays dry. "That is a bit late." is "È un po' tardi.", not a joke with a wink. No exclamation marks the English does not have.

## Words

- "Select" is "seleziona". Never "clicca" or "fai clic".
- Use the words Home Assistant uses in Italian: automazione, entità, dispositivo, area, piano, attivazione, condizione, azione, integrazione, stato. The glossary has the list.
- Home Assistant's Italian interface translates more than Spook's own strings do: a trigger is an "attivazione" (the button says **Aggiungi attivazione**), a dashboard is a "plancia", a blueprint is a "progetto", a template is a "modello", a helper is an "aiutante". Follow the interface, not Spook's strings.
- English words Italian Home Assistant users use stay English: script, bug, YAML.
- "Spook" is masculine and takes no article: "Spook segnala", "Spook è installato". Never "lo Spook", and never "il fantasma" for the product.
- "Home Assistant" takes no article: "Home Assistant si riavvia", "la versione di Home Assistant", "da Home Assistant". Never "l'Home Assistant".
- No em-dashes and no en-dashes. Use a full stop, a colon, a comma or brackets.

## Typography

- Quotes: "straight double quotes", like the interface. Never inside a component attribute like `alt`, where no quote of any kind is needed.
- Apostrophes straight: "l'automazione", "un po'". An accented capital is written with its accent, not with an apostrophe: "È", never "E'".
- No space before `:`, `;`, `?` or `!`.
- A sentence after a colon starts lowercase, unless it is a name.
- Capitals only at the start of a sentence and for names. Not in every word of a title.
- Numbers and versions as in the English. Times in `code` are not touched.

## Wording that repeats

Sentences that come back on every action page, written one way. Chosen for consistency on 2026-11-25 by an agent, not by a native speaker: the variant most pages used.

- "Just want to see it work?" is "Vuoi solo vederla funzionare?".
- "New to repairs?" is "Nuovo con le riparazioni?".
