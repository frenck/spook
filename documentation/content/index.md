# Spook 👻 Your homie

Hi! I'm Spook 👻 A scary powerful toolbox for Home Assistant, but don't be scared: I'm your homie.

I find the ghosts in your Home Assistant, like an automation still pointing at a device you removed months ago, and raise them on your repairs dashboard. And I bring the actions, triggers and conditions Home Assistant leaves out.
