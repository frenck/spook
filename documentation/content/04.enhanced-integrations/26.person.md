The person :term[integration] in :term[Home Assistant] tracks where the people in your household are. A person is a collection of device trackers that together say where that person is. A person can also have a user account attached, to log in to Home Assistant.

Spook adds actions to the person :term[integration] to change the device trackers of a person from an automation or script, and tells you when a person tracks something that is gone.

## Actions

Spook adds two actions for persons. You find them wherever you add an action, marked with a 👻.

### Add a device tracker

Adds one or more device trackers to a person.

The trackers of a person are set by hand on the People page, and stay that way. Real life does not. Somebody borrows the car with the GPS tracker for the weekend, or a kid takes the family tablet to school. With this action, an automation attaches the tracker to whoever has it right now, and [removes it](#remove-a-device-tracker) again later.

**How to use it**

1. Open the automation or script that should do this, and select **Add action**.
2. Search for **add a device tracker**, and select **Person: Add a device tracker 👻**.
3. Under **Person**, choose the person.
4. Under **Device tracker**, choose one or more device trackers.
5. Select **Save**.

Just want to see it work? [Try this action](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/developer_call_service/?service=person.add_device_tracker) opens it in your Home Assistant, ready to run once.

:::accordion-item{label="Example actions in YAML" icon="i-mdi-code-braces"}

Add one device tracker:

```yaml
action: person.add_device_tracker
data:
  entity_id: person.frenck
  device_tracker: device_tracker.iphone
```

To add multiple device trackers at once, use a list:

```yaml
action: person.add_device_tracker
data:
  entity_id: person.frenck
  device_tracker:
    - device_tracker.iphone
    - device_tracker.ipad
```

:::
:::accordion-item{label="Known limitations" icon="i-mdi-alert-outline"}

- Only persons made in the UI can be changed. One set up in YAML is refused.
- A device tracker the person already has is not added twice.

### Remove a device tracker

Removes one or more device trackers from a person.

The other half of [Add a device tracker](#add-a-device-tracker): the car is back, so its tracker no longer says where that person is.

**How to use it**

1. Open the automation or script that should do this, and select **Add action**.
2. Search for **remove a device tracker**, and select **Person: Remove a device tracker 👻**.
3. Under **Person**, choose the person.
4. Under **Device tracker**, choose the device trackers to remove.
5. Select **Save**.

Just want to see it work? [Try this action](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/developer_call_service/?service=person.remove_device_tracker) opens it in your Home Assistant, ready to run once.

## Repairs

Spook looks through your persons for device trackers that no longer exist, and tells you in the :term[repairs dashboard]{to="repairs"}. The repair names the person and the trackers that are missing.

This one Spook can fix for you, straight from the repair. New to repairs? Follow [Fix your first repair](/fix-your-first-repair).

### Unknown device trackers

A person tracks a device tracker that no longer exists, usually because it was renamed, or the integration providing it was removed. The person keeps working on the trackers that remain. So somebody whose phone tracker disappeared is just located a little less accurately.

::screenshot{name="repair-person-unknown-device-trackers" alt="The Unknown device trackers repair for a person still tracking an old phone, with the choice to remove it."}
An old phone that is gone, still on the person, and the offer to take it off.

::

**How to fix it:** Open the repair, and choose **Remove these device trackers** to have Spook take them off the person. Or choose **Let me fix it myself**, and change the person on the People page. If you want to keep them, **Keep them, stop telling me** does that. The repair disappears by itself once it is fixed.

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="How Spook checks this" icon="i-mdi-magnify"}

A person is a collection of device trackers, and the person's location is whatever those trackers say it is. Spook looks at the trackers of every person. When one of them no longer exists, it raises a repair that names the person and the missing trackers.

A tracker counts as existing when the entity registry knows it, or when it has a state right now. Many device trackers, like those reporting over MQTT or from the old `known_devices.yaml`, never get into the registry, so both are checked before one is reported. Spook looks again whenever an integration loads, the entity registry changes, persons are reloaded, or a device tracker appears or goes away.

Spook cannot edit a person set up in :term[YAML]. For those, the repair points you at your configuration.

:::

## Use cases

What people use this for:

- Add or remove a temporary device tracker for a person. For example, attach the tracker in the car to whoever took the car.
