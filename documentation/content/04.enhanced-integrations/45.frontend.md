A theme changes the colours of :term[Home Assistant]: the sidebar, the cards, the buttons, the dialogs. Home Assistant has one look of its own, and anything else comes in as a theme, written in YAML.

Spook brings a theme with it. No YAML, no files to copy. Install Spook, and **Spook** is in the theme picker.

## Spook theme

The colours of the ghost: its cyan, a cool gray called ink for everything in between, and red for when something went wrong. It has a light mode and a dark mode, and follows whichever one you use.

::screenshot{name="theme-spook-dashboard" alt="A dashboard in Home Assistant, wearing the Spook theme."}
A dashboard in the Spook theme. In dark mode, the ghost glows faintly at the top.

::

It goes all the way through: dashboards, settings, dialogs, forms, and the code editor. Buttons are cyan with dark text, like the ghost itself. Cards sit on a thin ink border. In light mode, links and selected items use a darker cyan, because the bright one is hard to read on white.

::screenshot{name="theme-spook-automations" alt="The list of automations in Home Assistant, wearing the Spook theme."}

::

**How to use it**

1. Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar, to open your profile.
2. In the **Theme** section, pick **Spook**.
3. Pick a **Theme mode**: **Auto** follows your device, or pick **Light** or **Dark**.

The theme you pick follows you to every device you log in on. Everybody in the house picks their own.

**Make it everybody's**

Somebody who never picked a theme gets the default one. To make that Spook, run the `frontend.set_theme` action once. [Try this action](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/developer_call_service/?service=frontend.set_theme) opens it in your Home Assistant. Pick **Spook**, or in YAML:

```yaml
action: frontend.set_theme
data:
  name: Spook
```

Spook remembers that choice, also through a restart of Home Assistant.

**On one dashboard view only**

Edit the dashboard, open the settings of a view, and pick **Spook** as its theme. That view wears Spook, whatever the rest of Home Assistant wears.

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="How it works" icon="i-mdi-cog-outline"}

Home Assistant has no way for an integration to add a theme: themes come from YAML. Spook adds its theme to the list Home Assistant reads them into, when Spook starts.

Reloading themes rebuilds that list from YAML, and the :term[Marketplace] does that after every theme it installs, updates or removes. Spook notices, and puts its theme back. When Spook was the default, that comes back too.

Already have a theme of your own called Spook? Yours wins, and Spook leaves it alone.

Remove Spook, and the theme goes with it. Anybody who had picked it gets Home Assistant's own look. Their choice is kept, so install Spook again and it is back.

:::

:::accordion-item{label="Known limitations" icon="i-mdi-alert-outline"}

- A theme only changes colours and a few shapes. Spook's own fonts cannot come along: a theme cannot load a font. Home Assistant's own font stays.
- Cards from the community pick their colours as they please. Most follow the theme, some do not.
- Themes change colours Home Assistant can rename between releases. Spook follows along, but a release of Home Assistant can show a spot that is not Spook-coloured until the next release of Spook.

:::
