Home Assistant Link, called Home Assistant Cloud before, connects your :term[Home Assistant] to cloud services like Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. It also gives you remote access to your Home Assistant instance, without dynamic DNS, SSL certificates, or opening ports on your router.

Home Assistant lets you turn those on and off in its settings. Spook turns them into switches, so a dashboard button or an automation can do it too.

## Devices & entities

Spook adds a device called **Home Assistant Link**, with a switch for each part of the connection: Alexa, Google Assistant, their state reporting, and remote access. Use them like any other switch: put them on a dashboard, or turn them on and off from an automation.

To find them, go to **Settings** > **Devices & services** > **Devices**, and search for **Home Assistant Link**. The switches are listed under **Configuration** on that device.

The switches only work while you are logged in to Home Assistant Link and connected to it. The rest of the time, they show as unavailable.

### Switches

#### Alexa

_Default :term[entity ID]: `switch.cloud_alexa`_

Turns the connection between your Home Assistant and Amazon Alexa on or off, all of it.

#### Alexa state reporting

_Default :term[entity ID]: `switch.cloud_alexa_report_state`_

Turns state reporting to Alexa on or off. With state reporting on, Home Assistant sends every state change of the entities you exposed to Amazon. That keeps the Alexa app up to date, and lets you build Alexa routines on those changes.

#### Google Assistant

_Default :term[entity ID]: `switch.cloud_google`_

Turns the connection between your Home Assistant and Google Assistant on or off, all of it.

#### Google Assistant state reporting

_Default :term[entity ID]: `switch.cloud_google_report_state`_

Turns state reporting to Google Assistant on or off. With state reporting on, Home Assistant sends every state change of the entities you exposed to Google. That keeps the Google Home app up to date, and lets you build routines on those changes.

#### Remote

_Default :term[entity ID]: `switch.cloud_remote`_

Turns remote access to your Home Assistant from the web on or off.

## Use cases

What people use this for:

- Turn off remote access while you are home, and turn it back on when you leave.
- Turn off Alexa while nobody is home.
- Turn off Google Assistant while nobody is home.
