The HomeKit Bridge integration in Home Assistant makes your entities available in Apple's Home app, and to Siri.

It picks those entities by their entity ID, and does not follow one that is renamed. Spook tells you when a bridge names an entity that is not there anymore.

## Repairs

Spook checks your HomeKit bridges for entities that no longer exist, and tells you in the :term[repairs dashboard]{to="repairs"}. New to repairs? Follow [Fix your first repair](/fix-your-first-repair).

### Unknown entities

A HomeKit bridge includes, or excludes, an entity that no longer exists, usually because it was renamed or removed. An included entity that is gone disappears from the Home app without warning, and an excluded entity that was renamed shows up in it again.

**How to fix it:** Go to **Settings** > **Devices & services**, open **HomeKit Bridge** (the repair links there), select **Configure** on the bridge, and pick the entities again. For a bridge set up in YAML, edit the `filter` of that bridge in your configuration instead. The repair disappears by itself once it is fixed.

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="How Spook checks this" icon="i-mdi-magnify"}

A HomeKit bridge keeps a list of the entities it includes, or excludes, by their entity ID. It does not follow an entity that is renamed.

Spook looks at the entities each HomeKit bridge includes and excludes, and raises a repair for a bridge naming entities that do not exist. Excluded entities are marked as such in the list. Bridges that are disabled are left alone.

:::

:::accordion-item{label="Known limitations" icon="i-mdi-alert-outline"}

- Only the entities a bridge names one by one are checked. Domains and glob patterns are not entity IDs, and are left alone.
- Per-entity settings in YAML (`entity_config`) are not checked.

:::
::
