The Switch as X :term[helper] turns any :term[Home Assistant] switch :term[entity] into a light, cover, fan, lock, siren or valve.

To Home Assistant, a smart plug is a switch. Correct for the plug, less so for the lamp plugged into it. The relay behind your garage door is a switch too. Switch as X makes a new entity of the kind you pick, and passes everything it does on to the switch underneath. Your lamp shows up as a light, with the light card and everything else that comes with being one.

That new entity only works as long as the switch behind it exists. Spook tells you when it does not.

## Repairs

Spook looks through your Switch as X helpers for a switch that no longer exists, and tells you in the :term[repairs dashboard]{to="repairs"}. New to repairs? Follow [Fix your first repair](/fix-your-first-repair).

### Unknown source entity

A Switch as X helper uses a switch that no longer exists, usually because the switch was removed or replaced. The helper has nothing left to switch, so it does nothing.

**How to fix it:** Open the repair, and Spook offers to remove the helper for you, once Home Assistant deleted the switch it is missing. One that may just not be there yet, like one of an integration that is still starting up, gets no button. A Switch as X helper cannot be pointed at another switch afterwards, so for a switch that was replaced: remove this helper, and make a new one for the new switch on the **Helpers** page, under **Settings** > **Devices & services**. Or bring back the switch that went missing. The repair disappears by itself once it is fixed.

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="How Spook checks this" icon="i-mdi-magnify"}

Spook reads the configuration of every Switch as X helper, and raises a repair when the switch it is set up with is unknown to Home Assistant. The repair names the helper and the missing source entity.

Spook reads the configuration, not the running entity. So a helper that failed to set up entirely is still checked, and that is the one most likely to be broken.

This is one check, shared by every helper built on another entity. The [Home Assistant page](/homeassistant#unknown-helper-sources) explains it in full.
