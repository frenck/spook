The media player :term[integration] is how :term[Home Assistant] controls speakers, televisions, receivers and everything else that plays.

Home Assistant has volume up and volume down, but how far those go is up to the player: its own step, or the press of its own button. Spook adds a step you choose.

## Actions

Spook adds two actions for media players. You find them wherever you add an action, marked with a 👻.

### Increase volume

Turns the volume of a media player up by exactly the step you give, stopping at full volume.

One speaker jumps ten percent on volume up, the next one two. A "louder" button that works the same on every speaker in the house needs a step you pick. Without this action, that is a template that reads the volume, adds to it, and keeps it under full. With it, it is one step.

**How to use it**

1. Open the automation or script that should turn it up, and select **Add action**.
2. Search for **increase volume**, and select **Media player: Increase volume 👻**.
3. Under **Targets**, choose the media player.
4. Set **Step**, in percent of full volume, like `5`.
5. Select **Save**.

Just want to see it work? [Try this action](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/developer_call_service/?service=media_player.increase_volume) opens it in your Home Assistant, ready to run once.

### Decrease volume

Turns the volume of a media player down by exactly the step you give, stopping at silent.

The "quieter" button to go with [Increase volume](#increase-volume). Also handy for a doorbell that should turn the music down a bit, not off.

**How to use it**

1. Open the automation or script that should turn it down, and select **Add action**.
2. Search for **decrease volume**, and select **Media player: Decrease volume 👻**.
3. Under **Targets**, choose the media player.
4. Set **Step**, in percent of full volume, like `10`.
5. Select **Save**.

Just want to see it work? [Try this action](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/developer_call_service/?service=media_player.decrease_volume) opens it in your Home Assistant, ready to run once.

Both actions work the same way:

- The step is required, in percent of full volume, and need not be a whole one: 2.5 is a fine step. A step that would go past full volume or silence stops there.
- Home Assistant's own `media_player.volume_up` and `media_player.volume_down` remain for when the player's own step will do.
- Muting is left alone: turning the volume of a muted player changes the level it comes back at.

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="Known limitations" icon="i-mdi-alert-outline"}

- A player with only volume up and down buttons, and no volume level to set, cannot be targeted.
- A player that does not report its volume, which many do while off, is left alone.

:::
::
