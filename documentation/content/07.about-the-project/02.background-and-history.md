I am [Franck Nijhof](https://github.com/frenck), better known as Frenck. I started Spook on [February the 23rd of 2023](https://github.com/frenck/spook/commit/67803faf19bca7c8543e0865e1dba58755315652).

## About the author

I am a :term[Home Assistant] enthusiast, and have been in the community since 2016. I am a member of the Home Assistant project, and have worked on many Home Assistant projects since.

In 2019, [Nabu Casa](https://www.nabucasa.com) hired me to work full-time on Home Assistant and related projects.

## How Spook was born

I review a lot of contributions, and am heavily involved in the architectural design of Home Assistant. Not every contribution, idea or design proposal makes it into Home Assistant.

That is not because they are bad. They might not fit the scope of Home Assistant, or they would put too much of a risk or burden on the project.

Whatever the reason, the community still wants some of those features to exist.

I felt the need to fill that gap and started working on it. Spook was born.

Spook is a :term[custom integration]{to="integration"} that fills that gap. It is a toolbox of features that are not part of Home Assistant itself, but are still useful to plenty of people.

Along the way, it also became a place for experimental features that might end up in Home Assistant one day. Usable, but not quite polished enough yet.

## Why the name Spook?

I am [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Netherlands) and grew up with "Casper het vriendelijke spookje", also known as <wiki:Casper_the_Friendly_Ghost>. "Spook" is the Dutch for "ghost".

Casper is scary at first sight, but you quickly get to like him. That fits a :term[custom integration]{to="integration"}: it is not reviewed or tested by the Home Assistant project, so being a little scared of one is not a bad thing.

"Your homie" is a reference to the live streams I used to do. I called my viewers "My Home Assistant Homies", or just "Homies". So it is about you, the Home Assistant user: its friend, its homie.

Nice little fact: it used to be "homey" at first (to maybe annoy the [Homey](https://homey.app) users a bit in SEO), but I decided not to be that _\*\*bad word\*\*_ and to change it back to just "homie".

Lastly, the little ghost logo and the emoji. That idea I took from the [🍄&nbsp;Mushroom&nbsp;card&nbsp;project](https://github.com/piitaya/lovelace-mushroom) (I love it! ❤️). They use a simple mushroom emoji, and you see it everywhere in the Home Assistant community. So I did the same.

## Goals of Spook

The goal of Spook is fairly simple: [**be the rebel**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/rebel).

Spook does not care about the rules, the architectural design or the philosophy of the :term[Home Assistant] project.

It aims to:

- Add new actions to existing Home Assistant :term[integrations]{to="integration"}, providing control of features you can use in your :term[automations]{to="automation"} and :term[scripts]{to="script"}.
- Inject new features into existing :term[actions]{to="action"} of Home Assistant integrations, adding new options to them.
- Add new entities to existing Home Assistant integrations, giving you more to monitor and control.
- Find what is broken or left behind in your Home Assistant, and raise it on your :term[repairs dashboard]{to="repairs"}, to help you keep things healthy, clean, and tidy.
- And more... nothing is impossible.

Spook uses almost any trick in the book to get the job done: undocumented features, private APIs, monkey-patching and workarounds.

Some features are not in Home Assistant for a reason, and you might see that differently. Spook doesn't care, and isn't here to judge. It is here to be your homie! And who knows, maybe some features turn out to be great after all, and end up in Home Assistant one day.
